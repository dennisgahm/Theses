What is Computer Science to Me (Dennis Gahm) Computer Science is something I studied at UCLA. I tried to master it. It gave me something to do. I played computer games as a child, and when I was very young, I believe I opened up something like a terminal and typed some text, but I didn’t know what it was or how to use it. There was a game, Rock Raiders by Lego that I played when I was young. I still remember it. “Rubble.” It was about drilling into cave walls and finding ores and crystals. You had to place certain structures like Starcraft onto the ground and have it fit just right. I really enjoyed that game. When I was at Thousand Oaks, California, I played Starcraft, but I started off with Fifa with my relative. He burned a CD with it and had a Lenovo laptop. He typed his credit card number into the computer with internet, but wanted to test what happened if he put one number incorrect. He would go to UCLA Computer Science’s PhD program before I went to UCLA - his name is Jin-kyu Gahm. When we played FIFA, he would tell me to rapidly press “w” for sprinting fast. Now that I think about it, holding down “w” may do the same thing, haha, but the former way may be more fun. Then I played Need For Speed Hot Pursuit 2. Jin Kyu Gahm would visit me later and tell me that I’m studied college level material for Computer Science at high school time, and that I’ll learn that in college because at the time, I didn’t understand it. I didn’t have the foundation to learn it, and the way to solve it at that time was just technique - I didn’t truly understand it.
