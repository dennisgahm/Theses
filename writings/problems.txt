<h1>Problems</h1>
There are many problems in life.
Some are computational, some are real-life.
Computers can aid in real-life problems,
but ultimately computer problems are abstractions of the real problems.
Computers are secondary, real-life problems are primary.

The are different types of problems.
The problems that are most important are spiritual problems.
These problems are the root and true substance of all problems.

Life and death, heaven and hell, the Bible and other religions -
these are big problems that span eternal principles.

Let us focus on Christ and His Word and gather at church,
and pray daily.

Let us worship the Lord, and give him glory everyday.

Let us sing and rejoice in every day and trial and joy.

Let us walk together this life, and enter into heaven together.
